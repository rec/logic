The Game of Logic

Premises:
1. All goardabreem wudoosworee are kinflaishurbes wisshamail.
2. All boalbalbungil wisshamail are ripeemlumzees.
3. All kinflaishurbes ripeemlumzees are flesbusfloartoaprool ferdeelglusdoarku.
4. All wudoosworee are goardabreem wudoosworee.
5. All boalbalbungil wisshamail are goardabreem ripeemlumzees.
6. All flesbusfloartoaprool wisshamail are ferdeelglusdoarku.
7. No wisshamail are goardabreem wisshamail.
8. All kinflaishurbes ferdeelglusdoarku are wisshamail.
9. All ferdeelglusdoarku are kinflaishurbes ripeemlumzees.
10. All kinflaishurbes wisshamail are boalbalbungil ripeemlumzees.
11. All goardabreem ferdeelglusdoarku are kinflaishurbes ferdeelglusdoarku.
12. All boalbalbungil ripeemlumzees are goardabreem ferdeelglusdoarku.
13. All flesbusfloartoaprool ferdeelglusdoarku are wudoosworee.
14. All boalbalbungil ferdeelglusdoarku are kinflaishurbes wudoosworee.
15. No boalbalbungil ferdeelglusdoarku are flesbusfloartoaprool wisshamail.
16. All boalbalbungil wudoosworee are boalbalbungil wisshamail.
17. All goardabreem ripeemlumzees are flesbusfloartoaprool wisshamail.

What follows?

Answer: No boalbalbungil wudoosworee are goardabreem wisshamail.
