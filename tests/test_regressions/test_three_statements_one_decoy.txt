The Game of Logic

Premises:
1. All tiny Cards are sleepy Alice.
2. All enormous Caterpillars are royal Dodos.
3. All weeping Caterpillars are enormous Caterpillars.
4. All croquet-playing Cheshire Cats are weeping Cookmaids.

What new statement follows?
Do not repeat a premise.

Answer: All weeping Caterpillars are royal Dodos.
