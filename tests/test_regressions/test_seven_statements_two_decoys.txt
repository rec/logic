The Game of Logic

Premises:
1. All tea-drinking Dormice are croquet-playing Dormice.
2. All late Hedgehogs are Eaglets.
3. All sleepy Duchesses are vanishing Gryphons.
4. All sleepy Cards are tea-drinking Dormice.
5. All late Kings of Hearts are late Hedgehogs.
6. No tiny Pigs are vanishing Queens of Hearts.
7. All croquet-playing Dormice are tiny Pigs.
8. All Eaglets are sleepy Cards.
9. No tiny Dormice are curious Bill the Lizards.

What follows?

Answer: No late Kings of Hearts are vanishing Queens of Hearts.
