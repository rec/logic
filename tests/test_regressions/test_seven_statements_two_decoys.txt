The Game of Logic

Premises:
1. All late Hedgehogs are Eaglets.
2. All tea-drinking Dormice are croquet-playing Dormice.
3. All Eaglets are sleepy Cards.
4. All tiny Pigs are vanishing Queens of Hearts.
5. All grinning Dodos are peppery Lories.
6. All late Hedgehogs are sleepy Cards.
7. No peppery Alice are late Cards.
8. All late Kings of Hearts are late Hedgehogs.
9. All late Kings of Hearts are Eaglets.

What new statement follows?
Do not repeat a premise.

Answer: All late Kings of Hearts are sleepy Cards.
